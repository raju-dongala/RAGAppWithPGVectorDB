Employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.

Annual leave must be applied through the HR portal.

Employees should submit leave requests at least 3 working days in advance.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Employees can download their salary slips from the Payroll section.

Employees can update their phone number and address from the Profile section.

Password resets can be performed using the Forgot Password option.
